Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Stock Score ("we", "us", "our"), developed by Sahid Khan, collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Stock Score mobile application (the "Application"). By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Usage and Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to understand how the Application is used. This may include the screens and features you interact with, app open and session frequency, device model, operating system version, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address, and crash and performance diagnostics. This data is not directly tied to your name or contact details.

1.2 Advertising Data

The Application displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device advertising identifiers (such as the IDFA on iOS or the Google Advertising ID on Android), IP address, and ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks) to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. On iOS, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before any tracking-related identifiers are used for advertising purposes; you can change this choice at any time in your device Settings.

1.3 Subscription and Purchase Data

In-app subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat. When you subscribe, RevenueCat assigns your device an anonymous app user identifier and records your subscription status, entitlements, purchase history, and renewal/cancellation events so we can unlock premium features and manage your subscription. Payment itself is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play — we do not collect or have access to your card or payment account details.

1.4 Referral Program Data

If you use the in-app referral feature, we generate and store a referral code linked to your anonymous RevenueCat app user identifier, together with a count of successful referrals, so that referral rewards can be attributed correctly. This is stored on our servers and locally on your device; it is not linked to your name, email, or other directly identifying information.

1.5 Support Communications

If you contact us for support (for example, by email or through an in-app "Contact Support" option), we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, the content of your message, and a support identifier that helps us locate relevant diagnostic information for your device.

1.6 Information We Do Not Collect

The Application does not require you to create an account or log in, and we do not knowingly collect your name, home address, or financial account details. Market data, stock scores, news, earnings, and dividend information shown in the Application are provided for informational purposes and are not linked to your identity.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Application's features, including stock data, screening, news, earnings, and dividend information
  • To process, validate, and manage subscriptions and entitlements
  • To operate the referral program and attribute rewards correctly
  • To display and measure the performance of in-app advertising
  • To understand how the Application is used so we can maintain and improve it
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse of the Application or referral program
  • To respond to support requests and communicate with you about the Application

3. Third-Party Services

The Application relies on the following third-party services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics) — app usage analytics and crash diagnostics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — app distribution and payment processing. See Apple's Privacy Policy or Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with these providers only as necessary for them to perform the services described above, or where required to comply with the law.

4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Application's features and for the purposes described in this policy, such as maintaining your subscription status and referral history. Analytics and advertising data are retained in accordance with the retention settings and policies of Google Firebase and Google AdMob.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier, or withdraw App Tracking Transparency permission at any time, in your device's Settings.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you by contacting us at the email address below.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, or applicable US state privacy laws, including the right to object to or restrict certain processing.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. International Data Transfers

Because we rely on third-party service providers such as Google and RevenueCat, information may be processed on servers located outside of your country of residence, including in the United States. These providers maintain their own safeguards for cross-border data transfers as described in their respective privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We rely on the security measures provided by our third-party service providers (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google Play) to help protect information transmitted through the Application. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Application or applicable law. We will update the "Last Updated" date below when we do. Continued use of the Application after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
